The blueprint

Based on Matt Smith’s method, with dosing from the stretching research.

1. SkillPick one position to train.
2. TestRate yourself on quick checks.
3. LimitersFind what’s really holding you back.
4. StackSmall → big, assisted + resisted.
5. Schedule1–3 focus sessions, rest between.
6. RetestEvery 4 weeks; re-plan.
