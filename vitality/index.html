The big rocks

Most results come from a handful of habits. In rough order of payoff:

  1. Sleep 7–9 hours on a steady schedule.[1] Short sleep shifts weight loss from fat toward muscle.[2]
  2. Lift 3–5 days a week, each muscle about twice,[3] most sets close to failure,[4] adding weight or reps over time.[5]
  3. Hit your protein — about 0.7–1 g per pound of bodyweight[6, 7] — and eat for your goal: a small surplus to build, a moderate deficit to lean out.[8, 9]
  4. Walk 8,000–10,000 steps a day,[10] including short walks after meals.[11]
  5. Do 2–3 cardio sessions a week. Fitness is one of the strongest predictors of a long life.[12, 13]
  6. Stretch with intent — short, frequent sessions add up.[14]
  7. Get morning daylight[15] and keep caffeine to the first half of the day.[16]
