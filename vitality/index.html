The big rocks

Most results come from a handful of habits. In rough order of payoff:

  1. Sleep 7–9 hours on a steady schedule. Short sleep shifts weight loss from fat toward muscle and drains daytime energy.
  2. Lift 3–5 days a week, each muscle about twice, most sets close to failure, adding weight or reps over time.
  3. Hit your protein — about 0.7–1 g per pound of bodyweight — and eat for your goal (small surplus to build, moderate deficit to lean out).
  4. Walk 8,000–10,000 steps a day, including short walks after meals.
  5. Do 2–3 cardio sessions a week. Fitness is one of the strongest predictors of a long life.
  6. Stretch with intent — short daily sessions to keep range, 2–3 hard ones to build it.
  7. Get morning daylight and keep caffeine to the first half of the day.
