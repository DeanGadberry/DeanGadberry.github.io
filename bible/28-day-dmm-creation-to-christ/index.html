28 Day DMM Creation to Christ

What to do each day

Read

  1. Pray and ask God to help you understand and obey what you read.
  2. Read the whole passage aloud, slowly.
  3. Read it two more times, noting what stands out.
  4. Close your Bible and retell the passage in your own words.
  5. Check your retelling against the text and fill in anything you missed.

Reflect

What does this tell us about God? What does he say and do? What does that show about his character?

What does this tell us about ourselves? What does it show about people, sin, and our need for God? Where do you see yourself?

What action should I take? Name one specific step of obedience and write it as an "I will" statement. Say what you will do and when.

Follow Through

Do your "I will" statement before starting the next passage. Before you move on, write down what you did and what happened.
