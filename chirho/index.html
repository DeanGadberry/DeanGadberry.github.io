Chi-Rho Cards are pocket-sized scripture memory cards, made to help you memorize whole books of the Bible one passage at a time — small enough to carry everywhere, clear enough to read at a glance. This is an early preview of the design; more books, translations, and formats are on the way.

Click or tap a card to flip it.

Scripture text is King James Version (public domain). Card designs and the Chi-Rho mark shown here are previews ahead of a first print run.
